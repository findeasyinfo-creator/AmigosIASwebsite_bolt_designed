## Design Principles

### 1. **Sophistication**
- Navy blue conveys trust, professionalism, and authority
- Perfect for educational institutions
- Premium feel for IAS coaching

### 2. **Elegance**
- Gold accents add luxury and achievement
- Represents success and excellence
- Traditional IAS colors (ceremonial gold medals)

### 3. **Modern Touch**
- Glass-morphism effects
- Gradient backgrounds
- Smooth animations
- Clean typography

### 4. **High Contrast**
- Navy + Gold = Excellent readability
- Accessible color combinations
- Professional color scheme
